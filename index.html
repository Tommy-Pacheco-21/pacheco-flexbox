Reflection: This was perhaps one of the easiest projects I've done so far, and a lot of that success can be attributed to the Flexbox Froggy Challenge. The website was extremely helpful in teaching me the basics of Flexbox commands, and was just a lot of fun blazing through the levels. In terms of difficulty, I had the most amount of trouble figuring out the differences between space-between, space-around, and space-evenly for justivy-content, and I was using these three modes one by one to figure out which looks the best. Overall, a very fun time, and I'll be most definitely utilizing Flexbox for future projects.
